Understanding Building Energy Ratings (BER)

What is a BER?

A Building Energy Rating (BER) certificate indicates the energy performance of a home. It is similar to the energy label on household appliances and rates the home on a scale from A0 to G, with A0 being the most energy efficient and G the least.

View official BER information and the current rating scale on the SEAI website

BER Scales

As of 24 May 2026, Ireland uses a simplified BER scale aligned with EU standards:

  • A0: Most efficient, zero fossil fuel use
  • A: Highly efficient
  • B: Good efficiency
  • C: Average efficiency
  • D: Below average efficiency
  • E: Poor efficiency
  • F: Very poor efficiency
  • G: Least efficient

The rating is based on primary energy consumption (kWh/m²/year) and carbon dioxide emissions (kg CO₂/m²/year).

When is a BER Required?

A BER certificate is legally required in the following situations:

  • Selling a property: All homes for sale must have a valid BER certificate.
  • Renting a property: All rental properties must have a valid BER certificate.
  • New homes: Newly built homes must have a BER before first occupation.
  • SEAI grant applications: Many SEAI home energy upgrade grants require a BER assessment before and/or after works.
  • Green mortgages: Some lenders require a BER for green mortgage products.

What Information Does a BER Certificate Contain?

A standard BER certificate includes:

  • The BER rating (A0-G)
  • Primary energy consumption per square metre per year (kWh/m²/yr)
  • Carbon dioxide emissions per square metre per year (kg CO₂/m²/yr)
  • An advisory report with recommendations for improving energy performance
  • Details about the property (type, age, floor area)
  • Date of issue and validity (typically 10 years)
  • A QR code linking to the detailed BER assessment record

How is a BER Calculated?

The BER is calculated using the Dwelling Energy Assessment Procedure (DEAP), Ireland's official method. A registered BER assessor will:

  • Conduct an on-site survey of the dwelling (typically 1-2 hours)
  • Measure dimensions, insulation levels, window types, heating systems, ventilation, and renewable energy technologies
  • Input the data into approved DEAP software
  • Generate the BER certificate and advisory report
  • Register the BER with the national BER register
