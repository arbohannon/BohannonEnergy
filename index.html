Our Three-Tier Service Approach

1. Domestic BER Assessment

Official BER certificate required for house sales, rentals, and SEAI grant applications. Includes professional explanation of results.

2. BER + Home Energy Advice

BER assessment plus consultation on priority improvements, grants, electricity tariffs, and recommended next steps.

3. Home Energy & Retrofit Plan

Detailed survey and tailored plan covering insulation, heating, solar PV, batteries, EV charging, sequencing, and contractor guidance.

About Bohannon Energy

I am Aiden Bohannon, an experienced energy assessor and former construction manager with over 400 DEAP assessments completed. I combine technical expertise with practical home energy knowledge to provide independent advice that helps homeowners navigate the complex world of energy improvements.
