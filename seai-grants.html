SEAI Home Energy Grants

Overview of SEAI Grants

The Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI) offers various grants to help homeowners improve the energy efficiency of their homes, reduce energy costs, and lower carbon emissions. Many of these grants require a BER assessment as part of the application process.

Main Grant Schemes

Individual Energy Upgrades

Grants for specific measures such as attic insulation, wall insulation, heat pumps, solar PV, heating controls, windows, doors, solar water heating, and battery storage.

Typical grant amounts: Varies by measure and property type (see table below for indicative amounts).

BER requirement: A BER assessment is often required before applying, and an updated BER may be needed after works to verify the improvement.

How BER Assessments Relate to SEAI Grants

BER assessments play a crucial role in the SEAI grant process:

  • Pre-works assessment: Determines the current energy performance of your home, helping identify which upgrades will be most effective.
  • Post-works verification: After completing upgrades, an updated BER assesses the improvement and confirms that grant requirements have been met.
  • One Stop Shop: Requires both pre- and post-works BER assessments to verify the achievement of the target B rating.
