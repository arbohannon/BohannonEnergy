# Bohannon Energy - Independent Home Energy Advisor Website

This is a static website for Bohannon Energy, providing independent home energy advice and BER (Building Energy Rating) assessments for domestic dwellings in Ireland.

## Website Purpose

The website promotes a sole trader home energy advisory business that offers:

1. **Domestic BER Assessments** - Required for house sales, rentals, and SEAI grant applications
2. **BER + Home Energy Advice** - BER assessment plus consultation on improvements, grants, and next steps  
3. **Home Energy & Retrofit Plan** - Detailed survey and tailored plan for energy improvements

The focus is on independent advice that helps homeowners make better decisions about energy improvements, particularly where they may be spending €10,000–€30,000 on upgrades.

## Structure

- `index.html` - Home page
- `about.html` - About the advisor (Aiden Bohannon) and background
- `services.html` - Details of the three-tier service model and advisory areas
- `contact.html` - Contact information and inquiry form
- `css/styles.css` - Stylesheet
- `js/main.js` - JavaScript for interactivity (contact form, active nav)

## Customization

To adapt this website for your home energy advisory business:

1. Update text content in each HTML file to reflect your services and details
2. Add your professional photo or logo in the `images/` directory
3. Adjust colors in `css/styles.css` if desired (look for color values)
4. Update your contact information in `contact.html`
5. Review and adjust meta tags for improved SEO
6. Consider adding testimonials or case studies (as images or text) if appropriate
7. Modify the indicative pricing in services.html to match your actual rates

## Note on Services & Qualifications

This website represents an independent home energy advisory service. While BER assessments require official registration with SEAI, the advisory services (grant guidance, tariff advice, solar/battery assessment, etc.) are provided based on experience and knowledge but do not replace the need for qualified contractors to carry out physical work.

When providing advice on SEAI grants, please clarify that you help homeowners understand eligibility and processes, but grant applications are submitted by the homeowner or their chosen contractor.
